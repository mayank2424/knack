# Knack: launch promo

**Goal:** Developers understand that Knack is a set of agent skills that *do the work and check it*, and they know where to get them.
**Platform:** Website hero, README, X. 1920×1080, 30fps, 16s, muted autoplay loop.
**Look:** Near-black `#0b0b0e`, warm paper `#f3efe6`, signal orange `#ff5a1f`. Instrument Serif italic for emphasis, Geist for UI, Geist Mono for commands. Precise and confident: `snappy` entrances, one spring on the logo.
**Audio:** None.

| # | Time | On screen | Motion | Why |
|---|------|-----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | 0.0–2.6 | "Give your agent a *knack*." | Words rise from masks with a 70ms stagger; "knack" lands in orange italic with a small spring | Hook: the name and the idea in one line |
| 2 | 2.8–9.4 | Six skills tick through one at a time: name, one-line job, category chip | A list column scrolls like a mechanical counter. The active row is full-bright, its neighbours are dim. Each holds 1.1s | Show the range fast without a wall of text |
| 3 | 9.6–12.0 | "Skills that *verify*, not just advise." | Line rises; a checkmark draws on next to "verify" | The differentiator |
| 4 | 12.2–16.0 | Knack wordmark + `/plugin marketplace add mayank2424/knack` | Mark pops (spring), command types out, then holds | CTA: where to get it |

**Flow:** everything enters from below and exits through the top. No hard cuts.
