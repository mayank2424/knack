Our story

Built by freelancers who were tired of chasing money.

We started Nimbus after a year in which three of us waited a combined 214 days for clients to pay invoices that were already overdue, and realised that the problem was never the clients themselves but the awkward, manual, easy-to-postpone process of following up. Nimbus takes that process off your plate entirely so you can focus on the work you actually enjoy doing.

Today Nimbus helps more than eleven thousand independent designers, developers, writers and consultants in forty countries send invoices that look great, get paid faster, and keep their books tidy without ever opening a spreadsheet at the end of the quarter.
